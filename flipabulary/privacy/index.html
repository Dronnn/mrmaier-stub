Privacy policy

Your words stay yours.

Effective date: November 7, 2026

Flipabulary is an offline vocabulary notebook. It does not require an account and does not operate a server for your vocabulary. This policy explains what is stored and what is not.

What Flipabulary stores

Your words, translations, examples, phonetics, parts of speech, stacks, tags, review history, learning settings, appearance, language, reminder preferences, and widget settings are stored on your device. They are used only to provide the app’s features.

What Flipabulary does not collect

  • No account, name, email address, or phone number
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
  • No vocabulary sent to a server
  • No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs

Widgets and local storage

Flipabulary and its widget extension share a local snapshot through an App Group so widgets can display your vocabulary and review workload. This snapshot stays on your device.

Notifications

Reminders are optional local notifications scheduled by the app on your device. Notification text does not include your words, translations, or due counts by default. No remote push service is used.

Purchases

Support tips are processed by Apple through the App Store. Flipabulary receives the purchase result needed to show confirmation, but does not receive or store your payment card details. Apple’s privacy policy applies to App Store purchases.

Backups

If you back up your device through iCloud or a computer, app data may be included in that backup. Backups are controlled by you and Apple, not by Flipabulary.

Children

Flipabulary does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app has no account system and no data collection.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
