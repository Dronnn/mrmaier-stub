Support

How can I help?

Flipabulary is made by one person. If something is unclear, broken, or missing, email dddron@gmail.com. Please include your device, iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem appeared.

Frequently asked

How do I add a word?

Open Words, tap the plus button, and enter the word and its translation. Examples, phonetics, part of speech, stacks, and tags are optional. Tap Add card to save.

How does review work?

A session shows one card at a time. Reveal the answer, then choose Again or Good. Good moves the word toward Learned. Again brings it back sooner. The exact steps, threshold, and decay rules live in Settings → Learning rules and in each stack’s settings.

What happens to my data?

Your words, stacks, tags, review history, and preferences are stored locally on your device. Flipabulary does not require an account and does not send your library to a server. See the privacy policy.

How do widgets and reminders work?

Summary and List widgets read a local snapshot shared with the widget extension. Reminders are optional local notifications scheduled on your device; no remote push service is involved. You can change or disable both in Settings.

What do the support tips do?

The Support screen offers six optional one-time tips: Coffee, Lunch, Big fan, Patron, Champion, and Legend. They are consumable purchases, can be repeated, and unlock no features. Learning, widgets, and reminders work without any purchase.

Can I restore a tip?

No. One-time tips are consumable and are not restorable. There is no subscription in Flipabulary, so there is nothing to cancel in App Store settings.

Why do prices differ by country?

The App Store sets local prices from the base price and local taxes. Flipabulary also uses lower price points in countries with lower purchasing power so the tips stay affordable.

How do I change the language?

Open Settings → Language and choose one of the 16 interface languages. Vocabulary you enter is never translated automatically.
