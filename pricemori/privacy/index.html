Privacy policy

Your journal stays yours.

Effective date: November 8, 2026

Pricemori is a private price journal for iPhone and iPad. It does not require an account and does not operate a server for your data. This policy explains what is stored, what leaves the app, and what does not.

What Pricemori stores

Your products, stores and branches, observations, prices, package sizes, conditions, notes, favorites, archives, and preferences are stored on your device using SwiftData and the app’s local preferences. They are used only to provide the app’s features.

What Pricemori does not collect

  • No account, name, email address, or phone number
  • No analytics, advertising identifiers, or tracking
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, microphone, or camera access
  • No price journal sent to a server
  • No third-party advertising or analytics SDKs

Network access

The app itself makes no network requests. Recording, charts, search, backups, and exports work offline. The only network activity comes from Apple’s App Store when you choose to leave a tip or when the system checks your App Store account.

Optional tips

Support tips are optional one-time purchases processed by Apple through the App Store. Pricemori receives the product and transaction information needed to show the price and confirm the purchase. The app does not receive or store your payment card details, and it does not send transaction information to a developer server. Apple’s privacy policy applies to App Store purchases.

Exports and backups

A JSON backup or CSV export leaves the app only when you explicitly choose to export it and pick a destination. Files you export are controlled by you and by the app or service you send them to. If you back up your device through iCloud or a computer, app data may be included in that device backup according to your Apple settings.

Notifications

Pricemori does not request notification access in this version. If a future version adds reminders, they will be local notifications scheduled on your device, and this policy will be updated before they ship.

Children

Pricemori does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. The app has no account system and no data collection.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
