Support

How can I help?

Pricemori is made by one person. If something is unclear, broken, or missing, email dddron@gmail.com. Please include your device, iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem appeared.

Frequently asked

How do I start my journal?

On first launch, add a product and record its first price. A product needs a name; brand, model, and notes are optional. Then choose a store or branch, the package size, and the amount. Pricemori keeps the product and branch selected for the next entry.

How do I record a price?

Open a product and tap Record a price. The date defaults to today and the previous package size is suggested. Add a card or multi-buy condition when the shelf price depends on eligibility or quantity. A shelf label is enough; you do not need to buy the item.

What does “No change” do?

It records today’s observation at the same price in one tap. This keeps the history honest: the price was seen again on that date. Undo is available immediately after saving.

How do I correct a mistake?

Open the product’s history, select the observation, and edit or delete it. Deletion offers Undo. Earlier entries are never replaced by a new one, so a correction changes only the entry you chose.

How are unit prices calculated?

Pricemori normalizes weight, volume, and item prices before comparing them. For example, 900 g for 300 AMD becomes 333.33 AMD per kilogram. Package shrinkage is handled the same way, so a smaller package at the same shelf price is visible as a higher unit price.

Why is a card or multi-buy price left out of rankings?

Those prices depend on eligibility or quantity, so they are not like-for-like with a regular shelf price. Pricemori keeps them visible but excludes them from regular rankings unless you compare the same condition.

Can I use more than one currency?

Yes. Pricemori supports AMD, USD, EUR, GBP, RUB, GEL, TRY, and JPY. A different currency is never converted automatically, and prices in different currencies are kept out of the same ranking.

How do I back up or export my journal?

Open Settings → Backup & export. You can save a full JSON backup, export a spreadsheet-ready CSV file, merge a backup, or replace the journal. Replacement is blocked until a safety copy has been exported.

What do the tips do?

The Support screen offers six optional one-time tips: Small, Medium, Large, Generous, Patron, and Extraordinary. They are consumable purchases, can be repeated, and unlock no features. Every part of Pricemori works without a purchase.

Can I restore a tip?

No. One-time tips are consumable and are not restorable. Pricemori has no subscription, so there is nothing to cancel in App Store settings.

Why do tip prices differ by country?

The App Store sets local prices from the base price and local taxes. Pricemori also uses lower price points in countries with lower purchasing power so the tips stay affordable.

Does Pricemori need a connection?

No. Recording, charts, search, and backups work offline. The app has no account and no server. Optional tips are handled by the App Store and need a connection, but they do not affect any feature.

What are the requirements?

Pricemori requires iOS 18 or later and runs on iPhone and iPad. The interface is in English. Appearance and text size follow the system settings.
